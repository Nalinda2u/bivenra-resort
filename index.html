01 — What we offer

Three ways to spend a day up here.

Everything at Bivenra is built around one idea: a private, beautiful place near Kandy where you can relax or celebrate without being disturbed. Pick the one that sounds like your day.

Riverside cottages & family cabins

Five and more cottages and family cabins, spread across the hillside so what you hear at night is the river, not the room next door. Guests keep telling us the same three things: quiet rooms, spacious suites, and a green view from the window.

  • Overnight stays for couples, families and groups
  • Day-use cottages if you want somewhere of your own without staying over
  • Room service and meals from our own kitchen

A pool with the mountains in front of it

Our natural mountain spring pool looks straight out over the ranges. Buy a day pass per head and swim all afternoon — you do not need to be staying the night to use it.

  • Dedicated private pool access for group bookings
  • Short eats, juices and cold drinks brought poolside
